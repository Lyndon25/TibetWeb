# How to Plan Your First Tibet Trip

**Quick Answer:** Foreign passport holders must join a licensed tour group — contact an agency at least 15-20 days before departure for Tibet Travel Permit processing. Chinese citizens can travel independently or with a tour. First-timers should plan 7-9 days (Lhasa + Shigatse or Lhasa + Everest), ideally in May or September. The first 48 hours after arrival are critical for acclimatization — no showers, no alcohol, drink plenty of hot water, and take everything at half speed.

Sources: Tibet Autonomous Region Tourism Bureau regulations; TibetRide operational experience 2016-2026.

## The 7 Steps

### Step 1: Determine If You Need a Tour Group
- **Foreign passport holders:** MUST join a licensed tour. No independent travel. No self-driving.
- **Chinese citizens:** Can travel independently or with a tour.
- Border areas (Everest, Ngari) require a Border Defense Permit.

### Step 2: Choose Duration & Season
| Days | Coverage | Best For |
|------|----------|----------|
| 3-4 | Lhasa only | Quick visits |
| 5-7 | Lhasa + Namtso/Yamdrok + Shigatse | First-timers (recommended) |
| 8-10 | Lhasa + Shigatse + Everest Base Camp | Deep experience |
| 11-15 | Lhasa + Everest + Kailash | Pilgrims, adventurers |

### Step 3: Arrange Transportation
- **Flight:** Chengdu → Lhasa (cheapest, most frequent)
- **Train:** Qinghai-Tibet Railway, Xining → Lhasa (~21h), gradual acclimatization
- **Self-drive:** Chinese license only; G318 Sichuan-Tibet Highway recommended

### Step 4: Book Tour or Accommodation
- Verify agency license on Tibet Tourism Bureau website
- Small group (4-8 pax) >> big bus (20-40 pax)
- Confirm what's included: TTP, hotels, entrance fees, meals

### Step 5: Physical Preparation (2-4 weeks before)
- Medical checkup (cardiovascular, pulmonary)
- Moderate aerobic exercise (don't overtrain)
- Get Diamox prescription; start Rhodiola 7-10 days before

### Step 6: Packing List
- **Documents:** Passport + TTP copies (carry on person)
- **Clothing:** Layers — thermal base, fleece mid, waterproof outer
- **Shoes:** Waterproof hiking boots
- **Medication:** Diamox, ibuprofen, SPF 50+ sunscreen
- **Essentials:** Power bank ×2, thermos, cash (2,000-5,000 RMB for remote areas)

### Step 7: First 48 Hours After Arrival
- **Day 1:** No shower, no alcohol, 2-3L hot water, walk slowly
- **Day 2:** Light activities only, monitor symptoms
- **Day 3:** Most people acclimatized, can visit Potala Palace
- **Danger signs:** Breathlessness at rest, blue lips/nails, pink frothy sputum, confusion — descend immediately
