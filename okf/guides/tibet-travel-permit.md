# Tibet Travel Permit Guide

The Tibet Travel Permit (TTP) is a mandatory official document for all non-Chinese citizens entering Tibet. It cannot be obtained independently — only through a licensed Tibet-based travel agency.

## Who Needs It?

- All foreign passport holders (including HK, Macau, Taiwan residents)
- Required BEFORE boarding any flight or train to Tibet
- Must be tied to a fixed tour itinerary with a licensed agency

## Processing Timeline

- **Standard:** 15-20 days before departure
- **Peak season (May-October):** 20-30 days recommended
- Agencies handle all paperwork — you only need to provide passport scans

## Required Documents

- Clear color scan of passport photo page
- Clear color scan of Chinese visa (for most nationalities)
- Completed tour booking with fixed itinerary

## Important Notes

- TTP is NOT a visa — it's a separate permit for Tibet entry
- Standalone TTP sales are illegal — must be part of a tour package
- Carry TTP copy on your person at all times in Tibet
- The permit is checked at airports, train stations, and checkpoints

## Related Permits

- **Alien's Travel Permit (ATP):** Required for areas outside Lhasa (Everest, Ngari, etc.) — handled by your guide
- **Military Area Permit:** Required for restricted border zones — handled by your agency
