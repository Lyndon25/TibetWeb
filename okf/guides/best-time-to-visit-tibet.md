# Best Time to Visit Tibet 2026

**Quick Answer:** The best time to visit Tibet is May and September — stable weather, peak scenery, and all routes are open. For budget travelers, November through February saves 30-50% on costs, and Lhasa's winter sun makes it feel warmer than the thermometer suggests. For specific sights: Nyingchi peach blossoms peak in late March to early April; grassland wildflowers bloom July through August.

Sources: China National Tourism Administration Tibet visitor statistics; TibetRide 2026 operational data.

## Quick Summary

- **Best weather + best scenery:** May to mid-June, or mid-September to mid-October
- **Lowest cost + no crowds:** November to March (winter), save 30-50%
- **Specific sights:** Late March to early April for Nyingchi peach blossoms, July-August for grassland wildflowers

## Best by Purpose

| Purpose | Best Months | Why |
|---------|------------|-----|
| Best overall | May, September | Best weather, best scenery, moderate crowds |
| Photography | April, October | Most dramatic light, richest colors |
| Budget travel | November-February | 30-50% lower costs, fewer tourists |

## 2026 Key Festivals

- **Tibetan New Year (Losar):** ~February 17, 2026
- **Nyingchi Peach Blossom Festival:** Late March - early April
- **Saga Dawa Festival:** ~May 31, 2026
- **Shoton Festival (Yogurt Festival):** ~August 20, 2026
- **Nagqu Horse Racing Festival:** Mid-August 2026
