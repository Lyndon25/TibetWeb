# How Much Does a Tibet Trip Cost in 2026?

**Quick Answer:** A Tibet trip costs USD 700-2,500 per person including round-trip flights. Budget big-bus tours start around USD 1,100 for 7 days, comfortable small-group tours (4-8 people) average USD 1,700-2,500 for 7-10 days, and luxury private tours run USD 2,800-5,600 for 12-15 days. The Tibet Travel Permit fee is always included in legitimate tour packages.

Sources: TibetRide 2026 actual tour pricing; Ctrip/Fliggy Tibet travel package data; CAAC flight price statistics.

## Three Budget Scenarios

### Budget (USD 1,100-1,400 / 7 days)
- Chengdu transfer flights, economy hotels, 5-day big-bus Lhasa tour, street food

### Comfort (USD 1,700-2,500 / 7-10 days)
- Direct flights, comfort hotels, 7-9 day small-group tour, mid-range restaurants

### Luxury (USD 2,800-5,600 / 12-15 days)
- Business class flights, St. Regis/Shangri-La level, private custom tour, fine dining
