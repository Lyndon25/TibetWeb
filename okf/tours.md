# Tibet Small Group Tours

TibetRide offers 4 small-group tours with native Tibetan guides. All tours include Tibet Travel Permit processing, English-speaking guide, private vehicle, hotel accommodation, entrance fees, and onboard oxygen.

## 5 Days Lhasa Essence Tour

- **Price:** from USD 598/person
- **Group size:** max 8 people
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Season:** April - October
- **Highlights:** Potala Palace, Jokhang Temple, Barkhor Street, Sera Monastery debate, Drepung Monastery
- **URL:** https://tibetride.com/tours/lhasa-5-days

## 7 Days Lhasa to Shigatse Discovery

- **Price:** from USD 823/person
- **Group size:** max 8 people
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Season:** May - October
- **Highlights:** Potala Palace, Yamdrok Lake (4,441m), Karola Glacier, Gyantse Kumbum, Tashilhunpo Monastery
- **URL:** https://tibetride.com/tours/lhasa-shigatse-7-days

## 9 Days Everest Base Camp Tour

- **Price:** from USD 1,034/person
- **Group size:** max 6 people
- **Difficulty:** Moderate
- **Season:** April - October
- **Highlights:** Potala Palace, Yamdrok Lake, Gyantse, Shigatse, Everest Base Camp (5,200m), Rongbuk Monastery
- **URL:** https://tibetride.com/tours/everest-9-days

## 12 Days Mount Kailash Pilgrimage

- **Price:** from USD 1,499/person
- **Group size:** max 6 people
- **Difficulty:** Challenging (52km trek at 4,600-5,630m)
- **Season:** June - September
- **Highlights:** Mount Kailash kora, Lake Manasarovar, Guge Kingdom ruins, Changtang wilderness
- **URL:** https://tibetride.com/tours/kailash-12-days

## What's Included in All Tours

- Tibet Travel Permit (TTP) + Alien's Travel Permit (ATP)
- Hotel accommodation (comfortable category)
- English-speaking licensed Tibetan guide
- Private vehicle with oxygen supply
- All entrance fees listed in itinerary
- Airport/train station transfers
- Emergency oxygen and first-aid kit

## What's Not Included

- Flights to/from Tibet
- Meals (except hotel breakfasts)
- Personal expenses and tips
- Travel insurance (required; must cover high-altitude activities)
