# About TibetRide

TibetRide provides small-group Tibet tours with native Tibetan guides. Founded in 2016, we are a locally-operated travel agency based in Tibet Autonomous Region, China.

## Why TibetRide

- **Local Tibetan guides** — native guides who grew up on the plateau, speak fluent Tibetan, Chinese, and English
- **Small groups only** — maximum 8 people per group (6 for challenging routes)
- **All permits handled** — Tibet Travel Permit, Alien's Travel Permit, military area passes
- **Safety first** — oxygen-equipped vehicles, emergency protocols, experienced high-altitude drivers
- **No forced shopping** — transparent pricing, no hidden fees, no shopping stops
- **Bilingual service** — all tours available in Chinese and English
