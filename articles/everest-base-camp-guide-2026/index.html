西藏珠峰大本营旅行指南 (2026年版)：站在世界之巅的脚下

Everest Base Camp Travel Guide 2026: Stand at the Foot of the World's Summit

首页 / 文章 / 珠峰大本营旅行指南 2026 Everest Base Camp Guide 2026

珠穆朗玛峰，藏语意为"圣母"，是无数旅行者心中的终极梦想。对大多数人来说，登上顶峰遥不可及，但前往位于西藏侧、海拔约5,200米的珠峰大本营（Everest Base Camp, EBC），则是近距离感受世界之巅震撼之美的绝佳方式。

本指南将为你详细解析从计划到实现的所有关键步骤，助你圆梦珠峰。

一、核心证件：入藏函与边防证

前往西藏珠峰大本营，证件是首要且必须解决的事项。

  • 入藏函（Tibet Travel Permit）：所有外国游客及台湾同胞进入西藏的必需文件。个人无法直接申请，必须通过西藏当地有资质的旅行社代办。
  • 外国人旅行证（Alien's Travel Permit）或军区批件：前往珠峰大本营等边境地区，在入藏函基础上还需办理此证。
  • 边防证（Border Permit）：所有游客（包括中国大陆居民）前往珠峰都必须办理。
    • 办理方式一（推荐）：户籍地派出所免费办理，目的地填"西藏日喀则地区定日县"，有效期3个月。
    • 办理方式二：由旅行社在拉萨或日喀则代办。
    • 2026年最新政策：自2026年4月15日起，大陆居民可通过"移民局12367"App在线免费申请电子边防证。

重要提示：

  • 务必通过正规旅行社安排行程，他们会全程指导并代办所有手续。
  • 所有证件必须随身携带原件，沿途有多处检查站。

二、最佳旅行时间

  • 黄金季节（4-6月 & 9-10月）：天气最稳定，能见度最高，是观赏和拍摄"日照金山"与"旗云"的最佳时期。
  • 雨季（7-8月）：云雾较多，看到珠峰全貌需要运气。
  • 冬季（11月-次年3月）：天气极寒，游客稀少，适合喜欢挑战和追寻宁静的硬核玩家。
  • 观看"日照金山"：能否看到全凭天气和运气，出发前建议查看云量预报。

三、如何抵达

从拉萨或日喀则出发，通常需要2-3天时间。

  1. 陆路包车/跟团（最常见方式）：从拉萨出发，经羊卓雍措、卡若拉冰川，抵达日喀则。从日喀则继续西行，车程约5-6小时。
  2. 换乘景区环保车：私家车不能直接开到核心区。到达换乘点后，必须换乘景区环保观光车（约120元/人）前往大本营。末班车约19:30。
  3. 沿途亮点：著名的"108拐"和加乌拉山口观景台（可远眺包括珠峰在内的5座8000米级雪山）。

四、住宿与餐饮

  • 住宿选择：
    • 大本营帐篷：海拔最高，观星和拍日出最方便。条件简陋，为大通铺（约80-120元/人）。夜晚极冷，高反风险大。
    • 山下巴松村/扎西宗乡：海拔较低（约4,300米），住宿条件更好，有供氧和暖气。强烈推荐给初次到访者。
  • 餐饮：以简单的泡面、蛋炒饭为主。建议自备巧克力、葡萄糖等高热量零食。

五、高原反应预防

  • 逐步适应：建议在拉萨（3,650米）停留至少2-3天适应海拔，再前往海拔更高的日喀则和珠峰。
  • 放慢节奏：抵达后避免剧烈运动、洗澡和饮酒。
  • 应对措施：轻度高反可通过休息适应，严重时务必租用制氧机吸氧，切勿硬撑。建议携带葡萄糖和布洛芬缓解头痛。

六、经典行程推荐

2天1晚经典行程：

  • Day 1：日喀则出发 → 经108拐、加乌拉山口 → 参观绒布寺 → 抵达大本营，拍摄纪念碑 → 夜宿帐篷或返回小镇。
  • Day 2：清晨拍摄"日照金山" → 返回日喀则。

七、必打卡亮点

  • 日照金山：日出或日落时分，阳光洒在峰顶的瞬间。
  • 珠峰测量碑：与标注8848.86米的海拔石碑合影。
  • 绒布寺：世界上海拔最高的寺庙，是拍摄珠峰的经典前景。
  • 世界海拔最高邮局：可购买明信片，盖上珍贵的珠峰邮戳。
  • 星空：远离光污染，用肉眼即可看见灿烂的银河。

八、常见问题 (FAQ)

Q: 外国游客可以去珠峰大本营吗？

A: 可以，但必须通过有资质的旅行社组团前往，并提前办好入藏函等相关证件。

Q: 去珠峰大本营需要徒步吗？

A: 不需要。西藏侧的珠峰大本营可直接乘车抵达，与尼泊尔侧需要多日徒步不同。

Q: 整个行程大概需要多少天？

A: 从拉萨出发，通常需要4-8天。建议至少预留5天时间。

总结

前往珠峰大本营是一次对身体和意志的挑战，更是一次心灵的洗礼。充分的准备是享受旅程的前提。

免责声明：旅行政策（如证件要求）可能随时变更，请在计划旅行时，务必通过官方渠道或合作的旅行社确认最新信息。

Mount Everest, meaning "Holy Mother" in Tibetan, is the ultimate dream for countless travelers. While reaching the summit is out of reach for most, visiting the Everest Base Camp (EBC) on the Tibet side at approximately 5,200 meters offers the perfect way to experience the awe-inspiring beauty of the world's highest peak up close.

This guide breaks down every key step — from planning to execution — to help you fulfill your Everest dream.

1. Essential Permits: Tibet Travel Permit & Border Permit

Securing the right permits is the first and most critical step for your Everest Base Camp journey.

  • Tibet Travel Permit: Mandatory for all foreign visitors and Taiwanese compatriots entering Tibet. Individuals cannot apply directly — you must go through a qualified local Tibetan travel agency.
  • Alien's Travel Permit or Military Permit: Required for border areas like Everest Base Camp, in addition to the Tibet Travel Permit.
  • Border Permit: All visitors (including mainland Chinese residents) must obtain one for Everest.
    • Method 1 (Recommended): Apply for free at your local police station. Destination: "Dingri County, Shigatse Prefecture, Tibet." Valid for 3 months.
    • Method 2: Have a travel agency handle it in Lhasa or Shigatse.
    • 2026 Latest Policy: Since April 15, 2026, mainland Chinese residents can apply for an electronic border permit for free via the "移民局12367" (Immigration Bureau 12367) app.

Important Reminders:

  • Always arrange your trip through a reputable travel agency — they will guide you through and handle all paperwork.
  • Carry original copies of all permits with you at all times. There are multiple checkpoints along the route.

2. Best Time to Visit

  • Peak Season (Apr-Jun & Sep-Oct): Most stable weather with the best visibility — ideal for viewing and photographing the "Golden Sunrise on Everest" and "banner clouds."
  • Rainy Season (Jul-Aug): Frequent clouds and mist — seeing Everest in full requires some luck.
  • Winter (Nov-Mar): Extremely cold with few visitors. Best for hardcore travelers seeking solitude and a challenge.
  • Golden sunrise viewing tip: Success depends entirely on weather and luck. Check cloud cover forecasts before setting out.

3. How to Get There

The journey from Lhasa or Shigatse typically takes 2-3 days.

  1. Private Car / Group Tour (Most Common): Depart from Lhasa, passing Yamdrok Lake and Karola Glacier, arrive in Shigatse. From Shigatse, continue westward for approximately 5-6 hours.
  2. Scenic Shuttle Bus Transfer: Private vehicles cannot drive to the core area. At the transfer point, you must switch to the park's eco-friendly shuttle bus (approximately 120 CNY/person) to reach Base Camp. Last shuttle departs around 19:30.
  3. Scenic Highlights Along the Way: The famous "108 Bends" and the Gyawu La Pass viewpoint, where you can see five 8,000m+ peaks including Everest.

4. Accommodation & Food

  • Accommodation Options:
    • Base Camp Tents: Highest altitude, best for stargazing and sunrise photography. Basic conditions — shared dormitory-style (approx. 80-120 CNY/person). Extremely cold at night with higher altitude sickness risk.
    • Basong Village / Zhaxizong Township: Lower altitude (approx. 4,300m), better facilities with oxygen supply and heating. Highly recommended for first-time visitors.
  • Food: Mostly simple fare — instant noodles, egg fried rice. Bring your own high-energy snacks such as chocolate and glucose tablets.

5. Altitude Sickness Prevention

  • Gradual Acclimatization: Spend at least 2-3 days in Lhasa (3,650m) to acclimatize before heading to higher-altitude Shigatse and Everest.
  • Take It Slow: Avoid strenuous exercise, showering, and alcohol after arrival.
  • How to Cope: Mild symptoms can be managed with rest. For severe cases, rent an oxygen concentrator — do not push through it. Bring glucose and ibuprofen for headache relief.

6. Recommended Classic Itinerary

2-Day, 1-Night Classic Itinerary:

  • Day 1: Depart Shigatse → 108 Bends & Gyawu La Pass → Visit Rongbuk Monastery → Arrive at Base Camp, photo at the monument → Overnight in tent or return to town.
  • Day 2: Early morning "Golden Sunrise" photography → Return to Shigatse.

7. Must-Do Highlights

  • Golden Sunrise on Everest: The magical moment when sunlight hits the summit at sunrise or sunset.
  • Everest Elevation Monument: Take a photo with the iconic 8,848.86m altitude marker.
  • Rongbuk Monastery: The world's highest monastery and a classic foreground for Everest photos.
  • World's Highest Post Office: Buy postcards and get them stamped with the precious Everest postmark.
  • Starry Sky: Far from light pollution, the brilliant Milky Way is visible to the naked eye.

8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Can foreign tourists visit Everest Base Camp?

A: Yes, but they must travel in a group organized by a qualified travel agency and obtain the Tibet Travel Permit and other required documents in advance.

Q: Do I need to trek to reach Everest Base Camp?

A: No. The Tibet-side Everest Base Camp is accessible by vehicle, unlike the Nepal side, which requires a multi-day trek.

Q: How many days does the entire trip take?

A: From Lhasa, it typically takes 4-8 days. We recommend allowing at least 5 days.

Summary

A journey to Everest Base Camp is both a physical challenge and a spiritual experience. Thorough preparation is the foundation of an unforgettable trip.
