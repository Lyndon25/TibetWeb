第一次去西藏？别慌，做好这7步就够了

第一次计划西藏旅行，很多人会感到信息过载——入藏函、高原反应、交通、住宿……太多未知因素让人焦虑。

作为在西藏运营近十年的本地旅行社，我们给出一条清晰的路线图：7个步骤，从下定决心到踏上高原。每一步都有具体操作指南，不废话。

第一步：确定你是否需要跟团

这是决定一切的第一步，也是最容易搞错的一步。

如果你是外国护照持有者（含港澳台）

  • 必须通过旅行社跟团。不是建议，是法律规定。
  • 不能自己办入藏函（TTP），不能自由行，不能自驾进藏。
  • 入藏函必须绑定持证旅行社和固定行程。
  • 至少提前15-20天联系旅行社办理。

如果你是中国大陆居民

  • 可以自由行，也可以跟团。
  • 自由行更灵活、更省钱；跟团省心、有导游讲解、有同行者。
  • 如果去珠峰、阿里等边境地区，需要边防证——在户籍所在地公安局办理（免费），或在拉萨通过旅行社代办（约100-200元）。
关键动作：如果你是外籍，现在就联系旅行社。入藏函手续需要时间——不要等到机票都买了才想起这一步。

第二步：决定出行时间和天数

时间和天数决定了你能去哪里、花多少钱。

天数能去哪里适合人群
3-4天拉萨市区（布达拉宫、大昭寺、八廓街）时间紧张、初次试探
5-7天拉萨 + 纳木错/羊卓雍错 + 日喀则第一次进藏的最佳时长
8-10天拉萨 + 日喀则 + 珠峰大本营深度体验、经典环线
11-15天拉萨 + 珠峰 + 冈仁波齐转山朝圣者、深度探险

第一次建议：7-9天。时间够走经典线路（拉萨+日喀则或拉萨+珠峰），也不会因为太久而疲劳。关于具体的出行月份建议，参考我们的西藏最佳旅行时间指南。

第三步：搞定进藏交通

方式一：飞机进藏（最主流）

  • 最佳中转城市：成都（航班最多、最便宜）
  • 拉萨贡嘎机场：距离拉萨市区约60公里，机场大巴25元约1小时到市区
  • 林芝米林机场：海拔更低（2,950m），如果想先适应高原再上拉萨，可以先飞林芝

方式二：火车进藏（最舒适适应）

  • 青藏铁路：西宁→拉萨，约21小时
  • 列车有弥散式供氧系统，海拔缓慢上升，身体有适应时间
  • 途经可可西里、唐古拉山口，窗外就是风景
  • 软卧约600-800元，硬卧约300-500元
  • 暑假期间（7-8月）一票难求，请提前至少30天抢票

方式三：自驾进藏（仅限中国大陆驾照）

  • G318川藏线（成都→拉萨）：最美，推荐首次自驾
  • G109青藏线（西宁→拉萨）：最经典，路况较好
  • G219新藏线（叶城→拉萨）：最艰难，不建议新手
  • 建议结伴出行，配备防滑链、氧气瓶和卫星通讯设备

第四步：预订旅行团或自行安排住宿

选择旅行社的标准（很关键）

  1. 是否正规持证：西藏旅游局官网可查。没有许可证的"黑社"办不了入藏函
  2. 团费是否透明：问清楚包含什么（入藏函？酒店？门票？餐食？），不含什么
  3. 小团还是大团：4-8人小团体验远好于20-40人大团，价格当然也更高
  4. 有无隐藏消费：正规旅行社无强制购物和自费项目。提前确认这一点
  5. 应急方案：问清楚如果高反严重怎么处理、有没有随车氧气

自己订住宿（自由行）

  • 拉萨：选择八廓街/大昭寺附近，走路就能逛。经济型150-350元/晚
  • 日喀则：市区酒店条件不错，120-600元/晚
  • 珠峰大本营：只有帐篷通铺，80-150元/人，条件艰苦但体验难得

第五步：身体准备——比你以为的重要

出发前2-4周

  • 体检：心脑血管、肺功能检查。有严重高血压/心脏病/哮喘的请遵医嘱
  • 锻炼：适度有氧运动（慢跑、游泳），但不要突击训练——体能和抗高反没有直接关系
  • 药物准备：咨询医生开乙酰唑胺（Diamox），预防高反。红景天需提前7-10天服用
  • 疫苗：无强制要求，建议流感+破伤风+甲肝

出发前1周

  • 停止剧烈运动，保证充足睡眠
  • 避免感冒——带感冒进藏是高反恶化的常见诱因
  • 减少烟酒
重要误区：体力越好≠越高反越轻。高反与年龄、性别、体能水平无关——奥运冠军和办公室白领在高原面前是平等的。

第六步：行李打包清单

证件类（最重要！）

  • 护照（外籍）+ 入藏函复印件（随身带，不要放行李箱）
  • 身份证（中国公民）+ 边防证（如去珠峰/阿里）
  • 旅行保险单（确认覆盖高海拔区域）
  • 备用证件照2张

服装类（分层穿搭）

  • 内层（排汗）：速干内衣、速干T恤 ×3-4
  • 中层（保暖）：抓绒衣、轻薄羽绒服
  • 外层（防风防雨）：冲锋衣（带帽子）、冲锋裤
  • 鞋子：防水徒步鞋（最重要的一件！）+ 拖鞋
  • 配件：遮阳帽、墨镜（高原紫外线极强）、保暖手套、围巾
  • 不用带正装——西藏哪里都是户外风

药品与防护

  • 乙酰唑胺（Diamox）/ 红景天 / 高原安
  • 布洛芬（缓解高反头痛）
  • 防晒霜 SPF50+（即使是冬天也必须涂）
  • 润唇膏、保湿霜（高原极其干燥）
  • 肠胃药、感冒药、创可贴
  • 晕车药（山路弯多，容易晕车）

其他必备

  • 充电宝：2个以上（偏远地区没插座充电）
  • 保温杯：高原喝热水极其舒服
  • 现金：2,000-5,000元（拉萨以外偏远地区微信支付可能用不了）
  • 湿纸巾：珠峰大本营没水洗澡，全靠湿巾
  • 零食：巧克力、坚果、能量棒——高热量的都带上

第七步：抵达后的48小时——最关键

到达拉萨的头48小时，决定了你整趟旅行的质量。

抵达当天

  1. 不要洗澡！热水让血管扩张，加重缺氧。忍一忍，明天再洗
  2. 不要喝酒！酒精加重脱水和缺氧，是高原最大的敌人
  3. 多喝热水：至少2-3升。注意是热水，不是冰水
  4. 慢慢走：说话慢、走路慢、一切动作慢一半
  5. 少吃多餐：消化会变慢，每餐七分饱
  6. 不要吸氧"预防"：如果没有明显不适，不要主动吸氧——让身体自己适应。有轻微头痛是正常的

第二天

  • 多数人会有轻度头痛、乏力——正常，多喝水、休息即可
  • 在拉萨市区轻度活动（八廓街散步、甜茶馆坐坐），不要去爬布达拉宫（台阶太多）
  • 如果头痛剧烈，吃一颗布洛芬；如果呕吐或症状加重，马上告诉导游或同伴
  • 继续不喝酒

第三天

  • 大部分人已基本适应3,650米
  • 可以去布达拉宫了（但爬台阶仍然要慢）
  • 如果感觉良好，第四天以后可以前往更高海拔地区
⚠ 危险信号（立即就医/下撤）：呼吸困难在休息时仍持续、嘴唇或指甲变紫、咳出粉红色泡沫痰、意识模糊或步态不稳。这些是高原肺水肿/脑水肿的征兆，需要立即医疗救助和下撤。

2026年最新提醒

  • 布达拉宫门票：旺季（5-10月）每日限流，必须提前预约。旅行社可以代订
  • 拉萨-林芝高铁：试运行中，开通后将大幅缩短交通时间
  • 5G网络：拉萨市区已全覆盖，偏远地区信号仍然有限
  • 电子支付：拉萨市区微信/支付宝全覆盖，但高原偏远地区仍需现金

常见问题

Q: 需要提前多久开始准备？

外籍游客：至少1个月（入藏函需15-20个工作日）。中国公民：前2周安排机票和酒店即可。旺季（7-8月）建议提前1-2个月预订。

Q: 一个人去西藏安全吗？

非常安全。详细分析参见我们的西藏安全指南。

Q: 到拉萨后能租摩托车自驾吗？

中国大陆驾照持有者可以租摩托车（需摩托车驾照D/E）。外籍游客不可以——入藏函要求全程跟团。如果你对摩旅西藏感兴趣，联系 TibetRide 了解摩旅团信息。

First Time in Tibet? Don't Panic — Just Follow These 7 Steps

Planning your first Tibet trip can feel overwhelming. Permits. Altitude. Transport. So many unknowns. You start Googling, and three hours later you're more confused than when you began.

As a local Tibet travel agency with nearly a decade of experience, here's a clear roadmap: 7 steps, from decision to plateau. Each with concrete actions — no fluff.

Step 1: Determine If You Need a Tour Group

This is step one for a reason. Get this wrong and nothing else matters.

If You Hold a Foreign Passport (including HK/Macau/Taiwan)

  • You MUST travel with a licensed tour group. This is not a suggestion — it's the law.
  • You cannot apply for the Tibet Travel Permit (TTP) on your own. You cannot travel independently. You cannot self-drive into Tibet.
  • The TTP must be tied to a registered travel agency and a fixed itinerary.
  • Contact a travel agency at least 15-20 days before your planned departure.

If You're a Chinese Mainland Resident

  • You can travel independently or join a tour — your choice.
  • Independent travel: more flexible, cheaper. Group tour: less hassle, expert commentary, travel companions.
  • For border areas (Everest, Ngari), you need an Alien Travel Permit (ATP) — obtainable at your local PSB before departure (free), or through a travel agency in Lhasa (~100-200 RMB).
Action item: If you're a foreign passport holder, contact a travel agency NOW. The TTP process takes time — don't book your flights first and realize this step is missing.

Step 2: Pick Your Travel Dates and Duration

Duration determines where you can go. Where determines cost.

DurationWhere You Can GoBest For
3-4 daysLhasa city (Potala Palace, Jokhang Temple, Barkhor Street)Time-crunched, first taste of Tibet
5-7 daysLhasa + Namtso or Yamdrok Lake + ShigatseIdeal first-trip length
8-10 daysLhasa + Shigatse + Everest Base CampClassic deep-dive circuit
11-15 daysLhasa + Everest + Mount Kailash koraPilgrims & adventure seekers

First-timer recommendation: 7-9 days. Enough time for the classic route (Lhasa + Shigatse or Lhasa + Everest), not so long you burn out. For specific month recommendations, see our Best Time to Visit Tibet guide.

Step 3: Book Your Transport to Tibet

Option A: Fly In (Most Common)

  • Best hub city: Chengdu (most flights, cheapest fares)
  • Lhasa Gonggar Airport (LXA): ~60km from downtown Lhasa. Airport bus: 25 RMB, ~1 hour to city center
  • Nyingchi Mainling Airport (LZY): Lower elevation (2,950m). Consider flying here first to acclimatize before heading to Lhasa — smart for altitude-worried travelers

Option B: Train (Best for Acclimatization)

  • Qinghai-Tibet Railway: Xining to Lhasa, ~21 hours
  • Onboard oxygen supply system. Gradual ascent helps your body adapt naturally
  • Route passes through the Kekexili Nature Reserve and Tanggula Pass — the scenery IS the journey
  • Soft sleeper: ~600-800 RMB. Hard sleeper: ~300-500 RMB
  • Tickets sell out fast during summer (July-August) — book 30+ days ahead

Option C: Self-drive (Chinese License Holders Only)

  • G318 Sichuan-Tibet Highway (Chengdu to Lhasa): Most scenic, best for first-time road-trippers
  • G109 Qinghai-Tibet Highway (Xining to Lhasa): Classic route, better road conditions
  • G219 Xinjiang-Tibet Highway (Yecheng to Lhasa): Most challenging — NOT for beginners
  • Travel in convoy. Carry snow chains, oxygen, and satellite communication

Step 4: Book Your Tour (or DIY Accommodation)

How to Choose a Travel Agency (This Matters)

  1. Licensed and registered: Check the Tibet Tourism Bureau registry. Unlicensed operators can't get you a TTP. Period.
  2. Transparent pricing: What's included? (Permit? Hotels? Entrance fees? Meals?) What's not?
  3. Small vs. large group: 4-8 person small groups offer a dramatically better experience than 20-40 person bus tours. More expensive, but worth it.
  4. No hidden costs: Reputable agencies don't do forced shopping stops or surprise surcharges. Confirm this explicitly before booking.
  5. Emergency protocol: Ask: what happens if I get severe altitude sickness? Is there oxygen in the vehicle?

Booking Your Own Accommodation (Independent Travelers)

  • Lhasa: Stay near Barkhor Street/Jokhang Temple — walk everywhere. Budget: USD 22-50/night
  • Shigatse: Decent city hotels, USD 18-85/night
  • Everest Base Camp: Shared dormitory tents only, USD 12-22/person. Rough but unforgettable

Step 5: Physical Preparation — More Important Than You Think

2-4 Weeks Before Departure

  • Medical checkup: Cardiovascular and lung function. If you have serious hypertension, heart disease, or asthma — consult your doctor.
  • Exercise: Moderate cardio (jogging, swimming). But don't over-train — fitness level has zero correlation with AMS susceptibility.
  • Medication: Ask your doctor about Acetazolamide (Diamox) for AMS prevention. For herbal alternatives like Rhodiola, start 7-10 days pre-trip.
  • Vaccinations: No mandatory requirements. Recommended: flu, tetanus, Hepatitis A.

1 Week Before

  • Stop intense exercise. Prioritize sleep.
  • Avoid catching a cold — entering Tibet with a respiratory infection is a common AMS aggravator
  • Cut back on alcohol and smoking
Important myth to bust: Being fit does NOT mean less altitude sickness. AMS doesn't care about your marathon times or gym routine. Olympic athletes and desk workers face the same hypoxia equally.

Step 6: Packing List — What You Actually Need

Documents (Most Important!)

  • Passport (foreigners) + TTP copy (carry on your person, NOT in checked luggage)
  • National ID (Chinese citizens) + ATP if visiting border regions
  • Travel insurance policy (confirm high-altitude coverage)
  • 2 spare passport photos

Clothing (Layer System)

  • Base layer (wicking): Quick-dry underwear, quick-dry tees ×3-4
  • Mid layer (insulation): Fleece jacket, lightweight down jacket
  • Outer layer (wind/rain): Waterproof shell with hood, waterproof pants
  • Footwear: Waterproof hiking boots (single most important item!) + sandals/slippers
  • Accessories: Wide-brim sun hat, UV-protective sunglasses (plateau UV is brutal), warm gloves, scarf/neck gaiter
  • Skip the formal wear — everything in Tibet is outdoor casual

Medicine & Protection

  • Acetazolamide (Diamox) / Rhodiola / Gaoyuan'an
  • Ibuprofen (for AMS headaches)
  • Sunscreen SPF 50+ (non-negotiable, even in winter)
  • Lip balm, moisturizer (the plateau is extremely dry)
  • Gastrointestinal meds, cold medicine, band-aids
  • Motion sickness pills (mountain roads = lots of curves)

Other Essentials

  • Power bank: 2+ (remote areas have no outlets)
  • Insulated water bottle: Hot water on the plateau is incredibly comforting
  • Cash: USD 300-700 (beyond Lhasa, WeChat Pay may not work everywhere)
  • Wet wipes: At Everest Base Camp, there are no showers. Wet wipes are your best friend.
  • Snacks: Chocolate, nuts, energy bars — bring calorie-dense stuff

Step 7: The First 48 Hours — Most Critical

Your first 48 hours in Lhasa make or break your entire trip.

Arrival Day

  1. Do NOT shower! Hot water dilates blood vessels, worsening hypoxia. Wait until tomorrow.
  2. Absolutely NO alcohol! Alcohol dehydrates you and worsens AMS — it's altitude's biggest enemy.
  3. Drink hot water: At least 2-3 liters. Hot, not cold.
  4. Move slowly: Talk slower. Walk slower. Do everything at 50% speed.
  5. Eat light: Digestion slows at altitude. Eat to 70% fullness.
  6. Don't use oxygen "preventively": If you feel okay, let your body adapt naturally. A mild headache is normal.

Day 2

  • Most people feel mild headache and fatigue — this is normal. Hydrate, rest.
  • Light activity in Lhasa (Barkhor Street stroll, sweet tea house), but do NOT climb Potala Palace (too many stairs)
  • If headache is intense, take ibuprofen. If vomiting or symptoms worsen, tell your guide or companion immediately.
  • Still no alcohol.

Day 3

  • Most travelers have largely acclimatized to 3,650m by now
  • You can tackle Potala Palace (but climb the stairs slowly)
  • If you're feeling good, higher-altitude destinations are safe from day 4 onward
⚠ Danger signs (seek medical help / descend immediately): Shortness of breath that persists even at rest. Blue lips or fingernails. Coughing up pink, frothy sputum. Confusion or loss of coordination. These are signs of HAPE (High Altitude Pulmonary Edema) or HACE (High Altitude Cerebral Edema) — require immediate descent and medical attention.

2026 Updates to Know

  • Potala Palace tickets: Daily visitor caps during peak season (May-October). Must reserve in advance. Travel agencies can handle this.
  • Lhasa-Nyingchi high-speed rail: In trial operation — will dramatically cut travel times when fully open
  • 5G coverage: Full coverage in Lhasa. Signal remains limited in remote regions.
  • Digital payments: WeChat Pay/Alipay accepted everywhere in Lhasa. Carry cash for remote areas.

FAQ

Q: How far in advance should I start planning?

Foreign travelers: at least 1 month (TTP takes 15-20 working days). Chinese citizens: 2 weeks for flights and hotels. For peak season (July-August), book 1-2 months ahead.

Q: Is Tibet safe for solo travelers?

Extremely safe. See our detailed Tibet safety guide.

Q: Can I rent a motorcycle in Lhasa?

Chinese license holders can rent motorcycles (need D/E motorcycle endorsement). Foreign passport holders cannot — the TTP requires you to be with your tour group at all times. If you're interested in a guided motorcycle tour in Tibet, contact TibetRide directly.
