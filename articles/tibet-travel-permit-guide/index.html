入藏函办理全指南：外国人进藏必备攻略

Tibet Travel Permit Guide: Everything Foreign Visitors Must Know

首页 / 文章 / 入藏函办理全指南 Tibet Travel Permit Guide

什么是入藏函？

入藏函，全称为"外国人入藏旅游批准函"（Tibet Travel Permit），是非中国公民进入西藏自治区旅游所必须持有的官方批准文件。根据中国国家旅游局规定，外国旅游者、台湾游客和海外华人（持中国特区护照或回乡证的香港、澳门居民除外）在藏期间不允许自行旅游，不论客人人数多少，必须有组织、有计划地组团旅游。

简单来说，入藏函就是针对非中国公民的"进藏签证"——即使你已经持有有效的中国签证，想要进入西藏，仍然需要额外办理这份文件。

自2007年7月1日起，外国人"旅藏确认函"的办理统一由西藏自治区旅游局行业管理处审核发放。

哪些人需要办理入藏函？

需要办理"入藏旅游批准函"的人群包括：

1. 非中国公民身份证持有者——中国台湾地区的游客；

2. 非中国护照持有者——外籍护照持有者。

特别注意：

  • 海外华人若无中国护照，也需办理"入藏旅游批准函"。
  • 现已取消港澳地区人员进藏旅游必须提供"入藏旅游批准函"的规定。港澳游客持中国特区护照、"港澳同胞回乡证"或"港澳居民来往内地通行证"均可自行赴藏旅游。

办理流程

申请进藏签证的手续并不复杂，主要包括以下步骤：

1. 将你的护照复印件及职业等相关信息交给办理旅游的旅行社；

2. 旅行社向西藏自治区旅游局提交申请；

3. 审核通过后，旅行社会给你一份进藏确认书（即入藏函）；

4. 只有凭借进藏确认书，才能购买机票或汽车票并随团旅游。

通常在2-4个工作日内可得到入藏的有关确认手续。虽然审核签发只需要3个工作日，但考虑到机票、酒店、向导与车辆等旅游事项的安排预定，建议尽早办理申请手续。

批准函注意事项

办理入藏函时需要准备以下基本信息：姓名、国籍、年龄、性别、职业、护照号码。同时提供进藏路线、大致游程时间、旅行意向、联系电话等资料，并将护照首页和签证的复印件提交给旅行社。

外国人前往西藏边境地区，可凭进藏批准函申请边境地区旅行证，一般由西藏旅行社代办。

重要提示：外国记者签证不允许进藏，持记者签证的外宾无法办理入藏函。外宾入藏一般需要办理旅游签证（L签证），商务签证也可以入藏，但流程相对复杂。

所需条件

不同类型的签证持有者，办理入藏函所需提交的材料有所不同：

  • 旅游签证（L）：只需提交护照和签证扫描件；
  • 定居签证（D）：须提交工作（或定居）证明；
  • 访问签证（F）：须提交中方邀请单位证明；
  • 职业签证（Z）：须提交聘用公司证明；
  • 学习签证（X）：须提交学校证明。

持公务签证和记者签证的游客不由旅行社负责接待，须向外事部门咨询相关情况。

入藏批件的保管：入藏批件在进入西藏后，交由随团导游保管；行程结束后，由旅行社统一交还旅游局存档。任何入藏批件均不允许被带离西藏。

相关签证种类

  • 旅游签证（L签证）：前往西藏旅游，请先与西藏当地旅行社（含涉外资质许可）联系申请入藏批准函。
  • 探亲签证（Q签证）：需提供亲属关系证明（护照出生地显示为中国者除外）。
  • 商务签证（F签证）：需提供《被授权单位签证通知表》或任职公司信函或内地单位邀请函。
  • 学习签证（X签证）：需提供《外国留学生签证申请表》（JW202表）和学校《入学通知书》。
  • 任职就业签证（Z签证）：需提供《外国人就业许可证》或《外国专家证》等。
  • 过境签证（G签证）：需持有前往国家或地区的有效签证及联程机票。
  • 外交/公务签证：需提供本国政府或国际组织的照会。
  • 乘务/机组人员签证（C签证）：国际交通工具乘务人员适用。
  • 定居签证（D签证）：须先获得公安部或公安厅颁发的《定居证》。

办理机构

西藏自治区旅游局办理"入藏函"的官方机构——拉萨办事处：拉萨市北京中路33号。

总结

如果你计划前往西藏旅游，入藏函是必不可少的通行证。请记住以下关键点：

  1. 外国人和台湾游客必须办理入藏函；
  2. 必须通过旅行社代为办理；
  3. 建议提前规划，尽早提交申请；
  4. 入藏后须随团旅游，不可独自行动；
  5. 入藏批件不可带离西藏。

What Is a Tibet Travel Permit?

The Tibet Travel Permit (入藏函), officially known as the "Foreigner's Tibet Travel Approval Letter," is a mandatory official document required for non-Chinese citizens to enter the Tibet Autonomous Region. According to regulations from the China National Tourism Administration, foreign tourists, Taiwanese visitors, and overseas Chinese (excluding Hong Kong and Macau residents holding Chinese SAR passports or Home Return Permits) are not permitted to travel independently in Tibet. All visits must be organized as group tours, regardless of group size.

In simple terms, the Tibet Travel Permit is an additional "entry permit for Tibet" — even if you already hold a valid Chinese visa, you still need this extra document to enter Tibet.

Since July 1, 2007, the issuance of the "Tibet Travel Confirmation Letter" for foreign visitors has been centrally managed by the Industry Management Division of the Tibet Autonomous Region Tourism Bureau.

Who Needs a Tibet Travel Permit?

The following groups are required to obtain a Tibet Travel Permit:

1. Non-Chinese ID card holders — visitors from Taiwan;

2. Non-Chinese passport holders — foreign passport holders.

Important notes:

  • Overseas Chinese without a Chinese passport also need to apply for the permit.
  • The requirement for Hong Kong and Macau residents to present a Tibet Travel Permit has been abolished. Visitors from Hong Kong and Macau holding Chinese SAR passports, "Home Return Permits," or "Mainland Travel Permits for Hong Kong and Macau Residents" may travel to Tibet independently.

Application Process

The process for applying for a Tibet Travel Permit is straightforward:

1. Submit a copy of your passport and your occupation details to a travel agency handling your tour;

2. The travel agency submits the application to the Tibet Autonomous Region Tourism Bureau;

3. Once approved, the travel agency will provide you with a Tibet Travel Confirmation Letter (the permit itself);

4. You can only purchase flight or bus tickets and join a group tour with this confirmation letter.

The permit is typically processed within 2-4 business days. While the review and approval itself takes only 3 working days, it is recommended to apply as early as possible to allow time for arranging flights, hotels, guides, and vehicles.

Important Notes on the Approval Letter

When applying, you need to provide the following basic information: full name, nationality, age, gender, occupation, and passport number. You should also provide your planned travel route, estimated trip duration, travel preferences, and contact information. Submit copies of your passport bio page and visa to the travel agency.

Foreign visitors traveling to Tibet's border areas can apply for a Border Area Travel Permit using their Tibet Travel Permit, typically handled by a Tibetan travel agency.

Important reminder: Journalist visas are not accepted for Tibet travel — foreign visitors holding journalist visas cannot obtain a Tibet Travel Permit. Foreign visitors generally need a tourist visa (L visa) for Tibet. Business visas may also work, but the process is more complex.

Required Documents by Visa Type

The documents required for the Tibet Travel Permit vary depending on your visa type:

  • Tourist Visa (L): Only passport and visa copies required;
  • Settlement Visa (D): Work (or residency) certificate required;
  • Visit Visa (F): Invitation letter from a Chinese organization required;
  • Work Visa (Z): Employment verification from the hiring company required;
  • Student Visa (X): School enrollment certificate required.

Holders of official/diplomatic visas and journalist visas are not serviced by travel agencies and must consult with foreign affairs authorities.

Permit custody: Upon entering Tibet, the permit must be handed over to the tour guide for safekeeping. After the tour concludes, the travel agency returns it to the Tourism Bureau for archiving. Tibet Travel Permits are not allowed to be taken out of Tibet under any circumstances.

Relevant Visa Categories

  • Tourist Visa (L): For Tibet travel, contact a local Tibetan travel agency (with foreign tourism qualifications) to apply for the permit.
  • Family Visit Visa (Q): Requires proof of family relationship (unless birthplace on passport shows China).
  • Business Visa (F): Requires an "Authorized Unit Visa Notification Form," company letter, or mainland organization invitation.
  • Student Visa (X): Requires the "Foreign Student Visa Application Form" (JW202) and school admission notice.
  • Work Visa (Z): Requires a "Foreigner's Employment Permit" or "Foreign Expert Certificate."
  • Transit Visa (G): Requires a valid visa for the destination country/region and a connecting/return flight ticket.
  • Diplomatic/Service Visa: Requires a note verbale from the applicant's government or international organization.
  • Crew Visa (C): For crew members of international transportation.
  • Settlement Visa (D): Must first obtain a "Settlement Permit" from the Ministry of Public Security or local public security bureau.

Issuing Office

The official Tibet Travel Permit issuing body — Lhasa Office of the Tibet Autonomous Region Tourism Bureau: No. 33 Beijing Middle Road, Lhasa.

Summary

If you are planning a trip to Tibet, the Tibet Travel Permit is an essential document. Keep these key points in mind:

  1. Foreign nationals and Taiwanese visitors must obtain a Tibet Travel Permit;
  2. Applications must be submitted through a travel agency;
  3. Plan ahead and apply as early as possible;
  4. You must travel in a guided group — independent travel is not permitted;
  5. The permit must not be taken out of Tibet.
