Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I book a tour?

A: Reach us via WeChat, email, or the custom trip form. Tell us your travel dates, group size, and preferences, and we'll recommend the best itinerary within 24 hours.

Q: How do I get the Tibet Travel Permit?

A: We handle the Tibet Travel Permit for you. Just provide clear scans of your passport and Chinese visa, and we'll take care of everything before departure.

Q: How far in advance should I book?

A: We recommend booking at least 2-4 weeks in advance. For peak season (May-October), 1-2 months ahead is ideal to allow enough time for permits and hotel reservations.

Q: Can I customize my itinerary?

A: Absolutely! All itineraries can be adjusted based on your interests, timeframe, and budget. Whether photography, trekking, or deep cultural exploration, we tailor it for you.
